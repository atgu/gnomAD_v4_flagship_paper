Identify all diseases associated with {gene_name} based on the provided articles.

Input:
- Gene: {gene_name}
- Articles: {articles_text}

Output a Markdown table with columns: `Disease | AssociationScore | Justification | PMIDs`

AssociationScore: 1 (strongest) to 4 (weakest) based on evidence strength.

If no phenotype caused by a mutation in {gene_name} is found, return: `| None | NA | [3-sentence summary of articles, stating no phenotype was identified] | NA |`
