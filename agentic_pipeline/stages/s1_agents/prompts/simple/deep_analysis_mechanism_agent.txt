Determine the pathogenic mechanism for each disease associated with {gene_name}.

Input:
- Diseases: {disease_list}
- Articles: {articles_text}

Output a Markdown table with columns: `Disease | Mechanism | ConfidenceScore | Justification | PMIDs`

Mechanism: GoF, LoF, DN, Conflicting, or Unknown.
ConfidenceScore: 1 (strongest) to 4 (weakest), or NA.
