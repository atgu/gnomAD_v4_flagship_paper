You are an experienced medical doctor reviewing medical procedure descriptors.
The objective is to find the procedures that have no reason to be enriched in a cohort of people with {endpoint_longname}, and to reject them (category rejected).
Any procedure that has a reason to be enriched in a cohort of people with {endpoint_longname}, shoul be kept (category selected).

Input (one per line):
{descriptors_text}

All inputs represent medical procedures. For each descriptor, decide:

- SELECTED: the procedure may be performed for evaluating, diagnosing, monitoring, or managing individuals with {endpoint_longname}. This includes procedures that may be used to confirm diagnosis, evaluate characteristic symptoms, monitor disease progression, or support therapeutic interventions. Maintenance of existing medical device that are commonly use in {endpoint_longname} is included, as well as research procedures in {endpoint_longname}. Will also be selected any procedure that may be performed for complications or symptoms of {endpoint_longname}. Nonstandard procedures are also selected.

- AMBIGUOUS: if unclear or insufficient context.

- REJECTED: the procedure is never used in clinical care for {endpoint_longname}. Procedures used for possible symptoms or comorbidities of {endpoint_longname} should NOT be rejected.

Note: it is okay to select all descriptors.

Output exactly three sections (no additional text), preserving input order:
SELECTED:
<descriptor>: <one-sentence justification>
AMBIGUOUS:
<descriptor>: <one-sentence justification>
REJECTED:
<descriptor>: <one-sentence justification>
